> **Summary:** Listing Alert Subscription Keyword Category  verification.  <br>

**Preconditions:** User is signed in and alert management screen is open.  

Scenario 1 

 | \# | Step | Expected Behavior |

 |  2 | Enter keyword 'Organic Rice' and select category 'Grains', then tap Subscribe. | Verify alert subscription is registered and displayed in the active alerts list. |  

**Post-conditions:**
